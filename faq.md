# FAQ

## What is eBridge?

eBridge, developed by the technical team of the aelf community, is the first user-facing decentralized cross-chain bridge within the aelf ecosystem. It successfully establishes bidirectional cross-chain connectivity between the aelf ecosystem and multiple other chains, achieved through innovations at both the protocol layer and web interface levels. One of the core themes in the current crypto market is cross-chain interoperability. A fully isolated blockchain ecosystem inevitably faces growth limitations in areas such as market capitalization, user base, and DApp diversity.&#x20;

The launch of eBridge provides the aelf ecosystem with a secure, fast, and low-cost cross-chain solution, enabling users to seamlessly transfer assets between aelf and multiple other chains in a trustless manner.

Undoubtedly, eBridge will play a pivotal role in breaking down the isolation of the aelf ecosystem and driving its integration into the broader multi-chain landscape.

## How Does eBridge Work?

eBridge deploys Bridge contracts and multiple oracle nodes on connected networks.

* For other chains → aelf transfers:
  * Tokens are locked in the other chain Bridge contract.
  * Oracles execute cross-chain data transmission.
  * Once at least 2/3 + 1 oracle nodes confirm the transaction, the aelf Bridge contract mints/releases an equivalent 1:1 amount of tokens to the user.
* For aelf → other chain transfers:
  * Tokens are locked in the aelf Bridge contract.
  * Oracles handle cross-chain data verification.
  * After obtaining signatures from all active oracle nodes, the other chain Bridge contract releases the native assets to the user at a 1:1 ratio.

## How do I initiate a cross-chain transfer on eBridge?

1. Select the "**From**" chain and "**To**" chain for the transfer.
2. Connect your wallet for the "**From**" chain and ensure the correct wallet is connected for the "**To**" chain. Alternatively, you can manually enter the receiving address for the "**To**" chain.
3. Enter the amount you wish to send, and confirm transaction details before pressing "**Bridge**".

## How to add/remove token pools?

Please follow the steps below:

1. On the "**Pools**" page, locate the token and corresponding chain you wish to add/remove, then click to proceed.
2. Select the **Add** or **Remove** tab.
3. Connect your wallet and ensure you have a sufficient balance.
4. Enter the amount you want to add/remove.
5. Click the **Add**/**Remove** button to complete the operation.

## How long does it take to complete a cross-chain transfer?

The confirmation time depends on the third-party network. You can check the estimated completion time for your transaction on the "**Transactions**" page.

## New Tokens

If you want to add a new token in eBridge, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have a balance of the token you want to add on the aelf chain.
2. Locate the "**Listing Application**" entry in the eBridge footer and click to proceed.
3. On the "**Listing Application**" page, connect your aelf wallet, select the token you want to add, and fill in the required token information.
4. Click Next, then choose the chain(s) where you want to list the token.
   1. If you have not listed this token on the selected chain(s) before, follow the prompts to complete the listing process.
   2. If you have already listed this token on the selected chain(s), proceed to bind it directly.
5. Click Next and wait for review by the eBridge team.

You can track the progress of your token addition via the "**My Applications**" page entry in the eBridge footer.

## What is the eBridge Telegram bot?

The eBridge bot is an official mini-App launched by eBridge on Telegram. Telegram users can use eBridge without downloading and registering aelf chain wallet, enabling asset transfers between the aelf chain and other chains. Currently, Telegram users can use all eBridge functions, including Bridge, and Info.&#x20;

Go to \[[https://t.me/eBridgeOfficial\_bot](https://t.me/eBridgeOfficial_bot)] and click "**Start**" or directly type "**/Start**" to see the current eBridge function menu.
